# Proposal

## Why

The Calendar and Cycle chart paint a derived status band only on days that happen to have a stored observation, so the band breaks into fragments with gaps wherever the user did not log. With a monitor Peak on day 12, the four fertile days the protocol guarantees (P+1 through P+4) stay blank because nothing was entered on them. The app already knows the answer; it simply refuses to draw it.

The gaps exist because the app chose to fill them with invented data instead: it synthesizes inferred Low records after the fertile window so those days would render. That workaround made stored records carry a `user`/`inferred` distinction, which in turn required a reconciliation pass on every write, a fill-mode setting, a suppression list for deleted rows, a provenance marker, and a nine-requirement capability spec — all to manage rows the app invented and then had to argue with the user about.

The rules already answer the question directly, so the app should derive the band from the rules and stop manufacturing records to justify it.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING** Derived status is painted for every day a cycle's window reaches, whether or not that day has a stored observation. The four post-Peak fertile days and the post-window days render as part of the band instead of as fragments.
- **BREAKING** The app no longer creates inferred day records. Nothing is synthesized, so no `dataOrigin` field, no post-Peak fill planner, and no reconciliation pass on write remain.
- **BREAKING** The `postPeakFillMode` setting and the `postPeakSuppressions` list are removed, along with their Settings controls and backup fields.
- **BREAKING** The confirmed/predicted `source` axis is removed across every surface. The Status badge, the Calendar full-detail source cue and legend entries, and the chart's dashed/solid window distinction are dropped, so a status band has one treatment rather than a predicted and a confirmed variant.
- An open cycle with no monitor Peak paints a derived band from the window begin through today, because the protocol leaves the window open until a Peak closes it. Future dates in that range are covered by the forecast treatment rather than the derived band.
- The forecast overlay applies only to dates after today. It previously also matched present and past dates in the newest cycle, which contradicted a derived band on those same cells.
- The algorithm toggle keeps its meaning but becomes simpler: turning interpretation off suppresses derived output only, with no stored records to hide.
- Autogenerated records already in a local database are deleted when the app loads that data, and are likewise not resurrected by restoring a backup taken before this change. No user-entered record is affected. This is a development-stage app with no compatibility commitment, so no data-version bump and no carry-forward of the removed settings fields are included.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `marquette-engine`: Day results cover the derived window rather than only recorded days; the evidence contract drops the confirmed/predicted source; the inferred-Low non-evidence requirement is removed; the post-Peak requirement no longer defines an inferred-record boundary.
- `post-peak-fill`: The entire capability is removed — all nine requirements covering provenance, suppression, fill modes, boundaries, reconciliation, and algorithm-off isolation.
- `calendar`: Cells derive and paint a status for any day inside a cycle rather than only for days holding a record; the autogenerated-record identification and reconciled-tail requirements are removed; the assumed-data marker and legend entry are removed; forecast is scoped to future dates.
- `status`: The view no longer reports a confirmed or predicted source, and the source-distinction requirement is replaced by a single status treatment.
- `history`: The per-cycle fertile-day count describes the cycle's fertile window rather than the subset of it that was logged, because day status is now derived for the whole cycle.
- `cycle-chart`: The fertile-window reference area no longer distinguishes confirmed from predicted windows, and per-day status covers unrecorded cycle days.
- `fertility-visuals`: The shared visual vocabulary drops the source cue treatments and the predicted-fill variants, leaving one treatment per status.
- `app-settings`: The post-Peak fill mode selector and the fill-behavior requirement are removed; the algorithm toggle requirement no longer describes generated-row hiding.
- `data-backup`: The backup document drops per-record provenance, and the preserve-identity requirement covers user-entered values without it.

## Impact

- **Engine (pure):** `src/core/engine/marquette.ts` builds day results from the window rather than from records and drops the `source` field; `src/core/engine/postPeakFill.ts` is deleted in full; `src/core/engine/types.ts` loses `DataOrigin`, `PostPeakInference`, `PostPeakSuppression`, and `PostPeakFillMode`.
- **Status derivation:** `src/core/cycleStatus.ts` returns a status alone.
- **Store:** `src/core/store/useAppStore.ts` loses reconciliation, suppression handling, and generated-row writes, and gains a one-shot purge of previously autogenerated rows; `src/core/store/selectors.ts` loses `recordsForMode`; `src/core/store/entities.ts` drops the provenance and fill-mode fields.
- **Features:** the Calendar grid, day cell, and legend lose the record gate, provenance, and source cues; the Cycle chart model and strip lose source handling; the Status card loses its source badge; the Settings Core section loses the fill-mode selector.
- **Visuals:** `src/lib/fertility-visuals.ts` drops the source cue map and predicted-fill selection; the corresponding design tokens in `src/index.css` become unused.
- **Backup:** `src/core/backup/backup.ts` stops reading provenance and fill settings; the backup format version stays at 1 because the change only removes fields and only ever exports the current shape.
- **Tests:** four `postPeakFill` suites are retired; engine, grid, chart, status, settings, and backup suites need updated expectations for the removed fields and the widened day results.
- **No new dependency.** No changes to the fertile-window begin/end rules, cycle placement, the algorithm toggle's default, or the sync-ready record identity scheme.
